## Google Business Profile eligibility assessment

Google requires an eligible business to make in-person contact with customers during its stated hours. An online-only personal brand is not eligible. A storefront must be a real, staffed location that receives customers and uses permanent signage; a virtual office is not eligible. A service-area business may qualify when it travels to customers, but it should use its real central location for verification and hide the address when customers are not served there.

The current evidence is not sufficient to create a personal Google Business Profile for Ibrahim. The website supports consultation enquiries, but that does not prove recurring in-person customer contact, a personal customer-facing location, stated staffed hours, or a qualifying service-area model. Do not create a profile unless all of the following are confirmed:

- Ibrahim personally makes in-person contact with customers during stated hours.
- The profile represents a real-world customer-facing practice, not a lead-generation or online-only identity.
- Any displayed address is a genuine staffed location with permanent signage where customers are received.
- Any service-area profile reflects real travel to customer locations and hides an address that is not customer-facing.
- The business name, telephone number, website, category, service area, and hours can all be verified by the owner.
- The proposed personal profile will not duplicate or misrepresent Code Moments or From Scratch.

Code Moments’ public website displays a business address and telephone number, so the company may warrant a separate owner-led eligibility review. That evidence does not establish eligibility for Ibrahim’s personal profile and is not authorization to create or claim either profile.

Official rules: [Business eligibility and ownership guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en-419) and [Guidelines for representing your business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en-en).
